      How it works

      • A seed word sits at the top. Every synonym of it you name is a 1st-order word, worth 8 points.
      • Once a word is on the board, its synonyms count as the next rung out: 2nd order = 4 points, 3rd = 2, 4th = 1. Nothing counts past the 4th rung.
      • A word always takes its shortest trace back to the seed. If a later find gives it a shorter route, it moves up a rung and scores more — a 4th-order word that turns out to be reachable in three steps becomes 3rd order. Extra links beyond that shortest trace are ignored, so there are no loops to untangle.
      • You get 20 entries. A word that lands and a word that misses each spend one; same-root words and repeats of what's already on the board are free. When the 20 are gone the round is over.
      • The clock starts on your first entry, not when the word appears.
      • A word that links to nothing — a miss or a misspelling — drops into tries and costs 1 point. There is no spellcheck and no hint about what went wrong.
      • A word is charged once. Anything on the tries list can be entered again for free, as often as you like — a word that linked to nothing early on may well land once the board has grown around it.
      • A word sharing a root with one already in play (discord and discordant are the same word) goes to same root instead. That bucket is neutral: no points, no penalty. It isn't a wrong answer, it just can't be its own answer.
      • A marks a looser link — the thesaurus data connects the two words, but not as a strict synonym.
      • The whole point is breadth at the first rung: eight 1st-order words beat sixteen 4th-order ones.
      • Simple, literary and erudite describe the seed word, not you. Rarer seeds have thinner thesaurus entries, so there is less to find and further to reach.
